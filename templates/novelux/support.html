Common Questions

How do I report a technical issue or bug? ▼

You can report technical issues by:

  • Emailing us at support@novelux.app with a detailed description
  • Using the "Report Issue" feature in your account settings
  • Providing your device info, app version, and steps to reproduce the issue
What's the response time for support inquiries? ▼

We aim to respond to all support inquiries within 24-48 hours. For urgent issues, priority support is available for premium members.

How can I recover my account? ▼

To recover your account:

  • Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password"
  • Enter your email address
  • Check your email for a recovery link
  • Click the link and create a new password

If you still can't access your account, contact support@novelux.app with proof of ownership.

How do I delete my account? ▼

To delete your account:

  • Go to your account settings
  • Scroll to "Danger Zone"
  • Click "Delete Account"
  • Confirm the action (this cannot be undone)
Can I get a refund? ▼

Refund policies depend on the type of purchase:

  • Coins/Premium: Refunds available within 30 days if unused
  • Books: Non-refundable once purchased and downloaded
  • Subscriptions: Cancel anytime; refunds available for unused portions

Contact support@novelux.app for specific refund requests.

How do I contact copyright concerns? ▼

For copyright issues, review our copyright policy and contact us at support@novelux.app with:

  • A description of the copyrighted material
  • The URL or content ID in question
  • Evidence of your copyright ownership
  • Your contact information
