Everything you need to know about your author benefits.

What does {{ b.revenue_share }}% Net Revenue Share mean?

You receive {{ b.revenue_share }}% of the net revenue generated by your story, calculated in accordance with your HuxNovel author agreement.

Can a short story qualify for both outstanding bonuses?

Yes. An eligible short story can earn both the ${{ b.short.0.bonus }} and ${{ b.short.1.bonus }} outstanding bonuses, for a total of ${{ short_total }}, when it meets each milestone's requirements.

What are the long-story completion requirements?

Your book must reach at least {{ b.long.min_words|intcomma }} original words and ${{ b.long.min_royalty }} in author royalty share, be marked as completed, and meet the applicable completion and eligibility requirements.

Is there a time limit for the ${{ b.short.1.bonus }} short-story bonus?

No. There is no time limit for the second milestone. An eligible short story qualifies when it reaches ${{ b.short.1.target }} in royalty share.

How do I get started?

Create a free author account, add your story and upload your chapters. Our editorial team reviews it, and books that perform well are invited to a contract. See the 5 steps →
